Senior Art Director & Photographer. Based in Bogotá, Colombia. I approach projects with a global vision, talking with the client to understand their brand and its DNA, focusing on what is the best way to implement an idea for the project, and how to translate it across various media: from web architecture and user experience design, to photography, illustration, and comprehensive branding.
This portfolio itself was built using vibe coding, combining AI-assisted development with my UX/UI expertise, and is fully optimized for both traditional search engines and AI-powered search.

Available for freelance — 2026

As a UX/UI designer, my goal is to transform a brand's vision into engaging digital and tangible experiences that are clear and authentic. I currently work as an independent freelance professional, helping my clients to tell their stories through design.

I'm now embracing vibe coding, using AI with my creative Art Direction skills to take them to the next level and build exceptional projects like this portfolio.

10+ years · 50+ projects · Bogotá → Worldwide

My work is an extension of my lifestyle: I am constantly learning about technology, tendencies, techniques. Always looking for references. I look for aesthetics everywhere, whether in the forms of nature, architecture, urban colors, pop culture or everyday details.

I strongly believe that constant learning is the best way to create good design.

The Fat Dragon — Themberchaud Concept Art

DIGITAL

A fan concept piece inspired by Alexander Ostrowski's latest designs, painted in Procreate on an infernal palette of indigo, burnt amber and crimson. Themberchaud, furious over a hoard of gold and grudges. A free take on the D&D dragon.

2024

Andean Fairy — What If Fairies Were Born in the Andes?

DIGITAL

What if the mythical creatures of medieval European fantasy had been born in the Andes instead? A dark-skinned fairy with white hair, owl-butterfly wings, aguayo textiles and a golden tumi in hand. Part of a series reimagining European folklore with Latin American roots. Procreate, amber palette.

Procreate · Latin American Fantasy · © 2024
Sep — 2024

Flawless Victory — Meninas de Canido 2024

DIGITAL

Official proposal for the Meninas de Canido street art festival in Ferrol, Galicia. Velázquez's Las Meninas reimagined as a tarot major arcana: a baroque border collie (a nod to "Canido") over a hypnotic cosmic spiral, flanked by golden Galician architecture that represents the revival of the Canido neighbourhood in Ferrol. Inspired by tarot cards, the creation process was meticulous; you can follow how the concept developed in the accompanying images. The piece was featured in the newspaper La Voz de Galicia.

Procreate · Official Proposal · © 2024 · La Voz de Galicia
Mar — 2024

Rocker Ghost Geisha — When a Japanese Yūrei Picks Up a Les Paul

DIGITAL

A Japanese yūrei geisha, lover and master of rock and roll, plays her Les Paul before a red rising sun. A purple-and-gold kimono, a Noh-mask face and a hair ornament. My source of inspiration was ukiyo-e woodblock printing, which I tried to give a rock twist. Procreate.

Procreate · Japanese Fantasy · © 2024

Reconciliación — Between the Living and the Lost

WATERCOLOUR · MIXED MEDIA

A pink-haired woman and a male figure revealing part of his skull hold each other's gaze: life and death, love and loss, the fragile and the fearsome. Chrysanthemums, ink splashes and geometric shapes frame the emotional core. It was my response to the theme "Reconcili-ARTE" of the IV Faber-Castell National Drawing Contest: it won second place and was exhibited at the Museo de Arte Contemporáneo de Bogotá, 2014. Watercolour and digital finishing.

2nd Place · Faber-Castell · Watercolour & Digital · © 2014
2019

Draconic Love — Commercial Dragon Illustration

DIGITAL · COMMERCIAL

A blue dragon and a red dragon curl into a heart around bold hand-lettered typography that plays on words: instead of "platonic love", it reads "Draconic Love". A commercial illustration created for a merchandise line: applied to apparel and accessories such as t-shirts and tote bags, plus an extra set of dragons for the Etsy shop profile. Vibrant pastel palette, fantasy character design and expressive lettering. Illustrator.

Illustrator · Merchandise Design · © 2019

Colibrí Lisérgico — A Heartbeat in Full Color

Benzaiten — The Goddess of the Arts, Updated

MIXED MEDIA
